Delete your data: Bunker Nights

App: Bunker Nights (Google Play)
Developer: Jesse Rances

There are three ways to delete data linked to your use of Bunker Nights. Option 2 works even if you no longer have the game installed.

1. In the game: delete your progress and cloud save

  1. Open Bunker Nights and go to Settings → Delete my data and cloud save.
  2. Confirm. The game then immediately and permanently:
    • deletes your cloud save stored with Google Play Games
    • deletes your saved progress and settings on this device
    • resets your Analytics ID, so new analytics aren't linked to your old data
    • signs you out of Google Play Games
  3. Purchases are not lost. They stay with your Google account and can be restored with Settings → Restore Purchases (or automatically after a reinstall).

Tip: If you also want your analytics and crash data deleted (option 2), write down your Analytics ID first. It's shown in Settings → Privacy choices, and this step resets it.

2. By email: delete analytics and crash data

Email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com with the subject "Bunker Nights: delete my data", and include your Analytics ID from Settings → Privacy choices. I will delete the Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics data linked to that ID and confirm by email within 30 days.

  • Without the Analytics ID I can't find your data, because the game doesn't collect your name or email.
  • If you no longer have the game or the ID, email anyway. I'll explain what can still be done, and analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months in any case.

3. Google Play Games data: managed in your Google account

Your Play Games profile, gamer name and any Play Games data Google holds for this game are controlled by Google. Delete them at play.google.com/games/profile → Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data (choose Bunker Nights under "Delete individual game data", or delete your whole profile). Instructions: Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile. Other Google activity: My Activity.

What's deleted, and what's kept

Data What happens
Cloud save (Play Games) and progress on the device Deleted immediately (option 1)
Analytics ID on the device Reset immediately (option 1)
Firebase Analytics data linked to your Analytics ID Deleted within 30 days of your email (option 2). Otherwise deleted automatically after 14 months
Crashlytics crash reports Deleted within 30 days of your email (option 2). Otherwise deleted automatically after 90 days
Purchase and order records Kept by Google Play under Google's policies. I keep sales records as long as tax and accounting law requires. Your purchases remain restorable
Aggregated analytics (totals and trends that don't identify you) Kept; they aren't linked to you
Advertising data (Google AdMob) Controlled by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage ads at My Ad Center
Your deletion-request email Kept only as a record that the request was completed, for up to 12 months

Response time

In-game deletion is immediate. Email requests are confirmed and completed within 30 days.

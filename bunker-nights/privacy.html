Privacy Policy: Bunker Nights

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

The short version
  • Bunker Nights ("the game") is a mobile game made by Jesse Rances, an individual developer ("I", "me"). The game doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts or photos. It has no chat, and I don't run game servers.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads, plus one full-screen ad at dawn before the results screen. If you buy "Remove Ads", the ad SDK isn't started at all once your purchase is confirmed.
  • I use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, they stay off unless you consent.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • Google Play Games sign-in is optional and never automatic. It's offered only after the privacy/consent step, and it's used for cloud save.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and older and isn't directed to children.
  • Settings → Privacy choices lets you change your choices and shows your Analytics ID. Settings → Delete my data and cloud save erases your progress. Full deletion steps are at https://ranceslabs.github.io/bunker-nights/delete-data.html.

1. Who I am

Bunker Nights is developed and published on Google Play by Jesse Rances, an individual developer based in Indiana, United States. I am the controller of the analytics and crash data described below; Google processes it on my behalf. For any privacy question or request, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is accessed, collected, used and shared when you play Bunker Nights on Android devices from Google Play, including through the third-party services built into the game. I'll update it before the game comes to other platforms (such as iOS).

3. Information kept on your device

  • Game progress and settings (nights survived, upgrades, Ecto balance, audio and accessibility settings such as the jump-scare toggle) are saved on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also stored in your Play Games cloud save.
  • Purchase status: Google Play confirms purchases on your device so the game can deliver them. Settings → Restore Purchases asks Google Play for items you already own.
  • Privacy choices: your consent and opt-out choices are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform, so they're respected every time you play.
  • The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Services that collect information

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

When you start the game, Google's consent and privacy message appears where the law requires it. It runs before any ads are requested, before analytics or crash reporting can start, and before Play Games sign-in is offered. It stores your choices on your device.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and a full-screen (interstitial) ad at dawn before the results screen. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general/teen audience. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

Data Details
Device identifiers Android advertising ID, app set ID
IP address Used to estimate general (approximate) location
Product interactions App launches, taps, ad and video views
Diagnostics App and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use

Google uses this data to show ads (personalized only where you've allowed it), to measure them and to prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads: once Google Play confirms that you own "Remove Ads", the game doesn't start the Google Mobile Ads SDK at all, so no ad data is collected from then on (rewarded bonuses are granted without an ad). Before the purchase is confirmed (for example, on a fresh install before your purchases are restored), the SDK may start as described above.

Google Analytics for Firebase (game analytics)

Collects an app-instance ID: a random ID for this installation, shown as your Analytics ID in Settings → Privacy choices. It also collects:

  • gameplay events (for example, nights played, ads watched, store screens opened)
  • in-app purchase events (product and price only, no payment details)
  • device model, OS version and language
  • approximate location derived from your IP address

I use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. Google processes the data on my behalf. I don't use it for advertising, and Google signals and data sharing are turned off. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, it stays off unless you consent.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

When the game crashes, Crashlytics collects a crash report: technical details of the crash (a stack trace), device model, OS version and a random Crashlytics installation ID. I use these reports only to fix bugs.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, it stays off unless you consent.
  • Crash reports created while consent is declined are deleted on your device without being sent, and they are never sent later, even if you consent afterwards.

See Firebase privacy information.

Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in and cloud save)

Sign-in is never automatic. The game offers it only after the consent/privacy step is complete, and you can decline. If you sign in, Google gives the game your Play Games player ID and gamer name, and your saved progress is stored by Google as a cloud save so you can restore it on another device. I don't create a separate account for you. Google may also collect usage and diagnostic data under its own policies (Play Games privacy).

Google Play (downloads and in-app purchases)

All in-app purchases (for example, Ecto packs, Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment information and order details under its own terms (Google Payments Privacy Notice). In the Google Play Console I may see order records (order ID, product, price, date, country or region). I use them only to deliver purchases, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting obligations.

Where the game uses Unity Technologies' in-app purchasing library to talk to Google Play Billing, that library processes limited technical diagnostic and performance data only briefly, to make purchases work. It isn't stored or shared, and it doesn't include personal identifiers or purchase history.

5. How information is used

  • To run the game: saving and restoring your progress and settings, including cloud save.
  • To show ads: personalized ads where you've consented or haven't opted out; otherwise non-personalized or limited ads.
  • To deliver purchases: delivering and restoring items you buy.
  • To improve the game and fix bugs: aggregated gameplay analytics and crash reports.
  • To prevent fraud: for example, Google's invalid-traffic detection and purchase verification.
  • To respond to you: if you email me, I use your address and message only to reply and to keep a record of the request.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example, tax records for sales.

6. Your privacy choices

  • Settings → Privacy choices reopens Google's consent or opt-out message, where you can change your choices at any time. It also shows your Analytics ID.
  • Advertising ID: in Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Google ad settings: control ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Play Games: sign-in is optional. Deleting your data in the game also signs you out of Play Games (section 10).

7. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform. It's a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

  • Ads: if you consent, Google may show personalized ads. If you don't, Google may show non-personalized or "limited" ads, and you can still play the whole game.
  • Analytics and crash reporting: these run only if you consent. If you decline, they stay off, crash reports from that period are deleted without being sent, and the game works normally.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. This doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew.

Legal bases:

  • Consent: ads that use device storage or personal data, analytics and crash reporting.
  • Legitimate interests: some ad-related processing such as fraud prevention, as described in Google's consent message.
  • Contract: delivering purchases and cloud save.
  • Legal obligation: sales and tax records.

Your rights: you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). To make a request, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com and include your Analytics ID from Settings → Privacy choices so I can find your data. For data Google controls (ads, Google Play, your Play Games profile), you can also contact Google directly.

8. US state privacy rights

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), Indiana, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon and other states may have the right to know about, access, correct and delete their personal information, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" or of targeted advertising.

  • Categories collected:
    • identifiers (advertising ID, app-instance ID, IP address, Play Games player ID)
    • internet or other electronic network activity (interactions with the game and with ads)
    • approximate location inferred from IP address
    • commercial information (in-app purchase events)
    • diagnostic and crash data

I do not collect sensitive personal information.

  • Disclosed to: Google, for advertising (AdMob); as my service provider (Firebase); and to provide Google Play and Play Games.
  • Opting out: in supported states, open Settings → Privacy choices to see Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option. You can also delete your advertising ID. Buying Remove Ads stops the ad SDK entirely.
  • Other requests: email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com or use the in-game deletion option. I may need to verify your request. You can use an authorized agent where state law allows. You won't be treated differently for exercising your rights.

9. Children

Bunker Nights is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In some regions the age of digital consent is higher, up to 16. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com and I'll delete the data I can link to them.

10. Data retention and deletion

How long data is kept:

Data Kept for
Game data on your device Until you use Delete my data and cloud save, clear the app's data, or uninstall
Cloud save (Google Play Games) Until you use Delete my data and cloud save, or delete it through Google
Analytics data (Firebase) Up to 14 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports that don't identify you may be kept longer
Crash reports (Crashlytics) Up to 90 days
Emails you send me (including deletion requests) As long as needed to handle your request; deletion-request emails are kept for 12 months as a record, then deleted
Purchase and order records Kept by Google under its policies. I keep sales records for as long as tax and accounting law requires
Advertising data (AdMob) Kept by Google under its retention policies

How to delete your data:

  1. In the game: Settings → Delete my data and cloud save permanently deletes your cloud save and the progress on your device, resets your Analytics ID, and signs you out of Play Games. This can't be undone. Your purchases stay with your Google account and can be restored with Settings → Restore Purchases.
  2. Analytics and crash data: email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com with your Analytics ID from Settings → Privacy choices. Note it down before you use the in-game delete, because that resets the ID. I'll delete the Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data linked to it within 30 days.
  3. Play Games data and profile: these are managed by Google in your Google account. See Delete your Play Games data or profile.

Full steps: https://ranceslabs.github.io/bunker-nights/delete-data.html. Your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android settings.

11. Security

All data the game and its built-in services send is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). I don't run my own game servers. Purchases go through Google Play's secure billing system. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I use reputable providers and keep the game's SDKs up to date.

12. International transfers

I'm based in the United States, and Google processes data in the United States and other countries. For data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google relies on legal transfer mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss extensions) and standard contractual clauses.

13. Sale of data

I don't sell your personal information for money. However, letting Google use your advertising ID and related data for personalized ads may count as "selling", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws. That's why the opt-out in section 8 is offered.

14. PC test builds

Pre-release PC test builds shared with invited testers collect nothing. They have no ads, analytics, crash reporting, purchases or sign-in, and they send no data. Log files stay on the tester's computer unless the tester chooses to send them to me.

15. Changes to this policy

I'll update this policy when the game's data practices change (for example, new services, new platforms such as iOS, or new features). The "Last updated" date will change, and for significant changes I'll give notice in the game or on the Google Play listing.

Privacy Policy – Ashdrift

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version
  • Ashdrift is a mobile game made by an individual developer, Jesse Rances. There are no accounts, logins, chat, cloud saves, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and I don't collect your name, email or other personal information through the game.
  • Your colony and game progress are saved only on your device.
  • The game offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob that you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus. There are no pop-up (interstitial) or banner ads. When the ad service runs, Google collects some device data (such as your advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • The one in-app purchase, "Skip the Ads" (a one-time purchase, $1.99 USD in the US), gives you every bonus instantly without watching an ad. It's processed by Google Play, and I never see your payment card details.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in Settings → Privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • The game is meant for players aged 13 and older.

1. Who I am

Ashdrift ("the game", "the app") is developed and published by Jesse Rances, an individual developer based in Indiana, United States ("I", "me", "my"). For privacy questions or requests, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is accessed, collected, used and shared when you play Ashdrift on Android devices distributed through Google Play, including the Google services built into the game. It will be updated before the game is released on other platforms such as iOS.

3. Information the app itself handles

I do not directly collect personal information. The game has no user accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting software, and I don't run servers that receive data from the game. - Game progress and settings: your airship, colony, colonists, resources, unlocks, statistics and settings (such as sound), plus whether you own "Skip the Ads". These are stored in a save file on your device only and are never sent to me. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes them, and there's no cloud backup from the game. - Purchase status: when you buy "Skip the Ads", the game receives a confirmation from Google Play on your device so it can unlock the purchase. It also asks Google Play for your existing purchases so the purchase can be restored after a reinstall. I don't receive or store your payment details. - Privacy choices: your ad consent or opt-out choices are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform so they're respected each time you play.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. If you allow notifications, the game may show a local reminder on your device (for example, when an expedition has finished). These notifications are created on your device and no data is sent to me.

4. Information collected by third parties

Google AdMob (advertising)

Ashdrift shows only rewarded ads: short ads you choose to watch, clearly labelled with the in-game bonus you'll get. You only get the bonus after Google confirms the ad was watched. Ads never appear on their own, and there are no interstitial (full-screen pop-up) or banner ads. According to Google's documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

Data Details
Device and account identifiers Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device
IP address May be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device
Product interactions For example, app launches, taps, and ad and video views
Diagnostic information App and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use

Ad requests also include basic technical information (device type, operating system, language, app version) so a compatible ad can be delivered. Google uses this data for advertising (including personalized ads where you have allowed it), measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. For this data Google acts as an independent controller (Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms). Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services · Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

"Skip the Ads" purchase: if you buy "Skip the Ads", every bonus that is normally offered for watching a rewarded ad is granted instantly, without an ad. Once your purchase is confirmed by Google Play, the game no longer starts the ad service or requests ads, so the Google Mobile Ads SDK no longer sends ad requests from your device. Before the purchase is confirmed (for example on first launch after a reinstall, before the purchase is restored), the ad service may start as described above. The SDK is still included in the app, so the consent and privacy choices in sections 6 to 8 remain available.

Google Play (downloads and in-app purchase)

Ashdrift is distributed through Google Play, and its in-app purchase, "Skip the Ads" (a one-time, non-consumable purchase), is processed by Google Play Billing. Google collects your payment information, order details and purchase tokens under its own terms. I may see order records in Google Play Console (order ID, product, price, date, country or region) and use them only to provide the purchase, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting obligations. Google Payments Privacy Notice. The game contains no random or "loot box" purchases.

Google Play and Android may also give developers aggregated statistics and crash or performance reports (Android vitals) from users who chose to share usage and diagnostics with Google. The game itself adds no separate crash-reporting or analytics software.

5. How information is used

  • To run the game: saving progress and settings on your device.
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch: personalized where you have consented or not opted out, and non-personalized or limited otherwise.
  • To deliver and restore your purchase.
  • To prevent fraud and keep things working, for example Google's invalid-traffic detection and purchase verification by Google Play.
  • To reply if you email me.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax records or valid legal requests.

6. Your ad privacy choices

  • In-game: where required, the game shows a consent or privacy message from Google's User Messaging Platform when it starts, before any ads are requested. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Simply don't watch ads: rewarded ads are always optional. Or buy "Skip the Ads" to get every bonus without ads.
  • Advertising ID: go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device) to reset or delete it.
  • Google ad settings: My Ad Center.

7. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. It asks for your consent before Google stores or accesses information on your device and uses personal data for ads. If you don't consent, Google may show non-personalized or "limited" ads, and you can still play the full game. You can withdraw or change consent at any time in Settings → Privacy choices; withdrawal doesn't affect earlier processing. Legal bases: consent (ad-related device storage and personalization); legitimate interests as described in Google's consent message (for example fraud prevention and basic measurement); contract (delivering your purchase); legal obligation (tax records). Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawing consent, and complaining to your data protection authority. I hold no player data beyond emails you send me. For data Google holds, use Google's tools or contact me and I'll help where I can.

8. US state privacy rights (California, Indiana and other states)

  • Categories: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (ad and app interactions), and approximate geolocation inferred from IP address. These are disclosed to Google for the advertising purposes above when ads are requested. No sensitive personal information is collected.
  • Opting out of sale, sharing or targeted ads: in supported states, Settings → Privacy choices shows Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" message. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Other requests: email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, I may not be able to link a request to you. Authorized agents are allowed where the law permits, and I may need to verify requests. You won't be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

9. Children

Ashdrift is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (as defined by COPPA). In some regions the age of digital consent is higher, up to 16. If you believe a child under 13 has given me personal information (for example by email), contact ranceslabs.support@gmail.com and I will delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device game data stays until you delete it: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Ashdrift → Storage → Clear data. This also resets your local privacy choices.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted. You can ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google. I may keep sales records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Google-collected data is retained under Google's policies.

11. Security

The game sends no personal information to my own servers. According to Google, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS), and purchases use Google Play's billing system. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

12. International transfers

I'm based in the United States. Google processes data in the US and elsewhere and relies on legal mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses (Google privacy frameworks).

13. Sale of data

I don't sell personal information for money. However, letting Google use your advertising ID and related data for personalized ads may count as "selling", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws, which is why the opt-out in section 8 exists.

14. Changes

I'll update this policy before adding features that change data practices, such as an iOS version, analytics, cloud saves or new purchases. The "Last updated" date will change, and significant changes will be noted in the game or on the Google Play listing.

Delete your data – Last Bastion

Last Bastion by Jesse Rances (individual developer) · Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version
  • Last Bastion has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud saves. There is no account to delete.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. It is never sent to me.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it permanently.
  • For anything else, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com.

1. Delete your game data

Game progress and settings (shards, upgrades, best wave, run statistics, sound and speed settings, and whether you own "Remove Ads") are stored in a save file on your device only. To delete them, do either of the following:

  1. Uninstall Last Bastion from your device, or
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Last Bastion → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device).

This permanently erases your local progress and also removes your locally stored ad privacy choices, so you'll be asked again where required. It can't be undone, because there is no backup. A "Remove Ads" purchase is kept by Google Play and is restored when you reinstall the game with the same Google account.

2. Reset or delete your advertising ID

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google controls that data, so I can't delete it for you, but you can:

  • Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. If it is deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID.
  • Control ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.
  • See How Google retains data and the Google Privacy Policy to exercise your rights directly with Google.

3. Change your ad consent

Open the game and go to Settings → Privacy choices. This reopens Google's privacy message, where you can:

  • give or withdraw consent for personalized ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland), and
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information or targeted advertising (supported US states, through Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" message).

You can still play the full game whatever you choose.

4. Email any other request

For any other privacy or deletion request, including deleting emails you've sent me, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com with "Last Bastion" in the subject. Because the game has no accounts and I don't hold player data, I may not be able to link a request to you. For data held by Google, you may also contact Google directly.

What is kept

  • Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted. You can ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase records in Google Play Console are kept by Google. I may retain sales records as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Data collected by Google (AdMob and Google Play) is retained under Google's policies.

See the full privacy policy for details.

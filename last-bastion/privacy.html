Privacy Policy – Last Bastion

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version
  • Last Bastion is a mobile game made by an individual developer, Jesse Rances. I do not run accounts, logins, chat, or cloud saves, and I do not collect your name, email, or other personal information through the game.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data, such as your advertising ID, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud.
  • Purchases, such as "Remove Ads", are processed by Google Play. I never see your payment card details.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game, and you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in Android settings.
  • The game is meant for players aged 13 and older.

1. Who I am

Last Bastion ("the game", "the app") is developed and published by Jesse Rances, an individual developer based in Indiana, United States ("I", "me", "my"). For any privacy question or request, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is accessed, collected, used and shared when you play Last Bastion on Android devices distributed through Google Play. It also covers the third-party services built into the game. It will be updated before the game is released on other platforms, such as iOS.

3. Information the app itself handles

I do not directly collect personal information. The game has no user accounts, sign-in, chat, or social features, and I do not run any servers that receive data from the game.

  • Game progress and settings: shards, upgrades, best wave, run statistics, sound and speed settings, and whether you own "Remove Ads". These are stored in a save file on your device only. They are not sent to me. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes them.
  • Purchase status: when you buy something, the game receives a confirmation from Google Play on your device so it can unlock the item. The game also asks Google Play for your existing purchases so "Remove Ads" can be restored after a reinstall. I don't receive or store your payment details.
  • Privacy choices: your consent or opt-out choices for ads are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform so they are respected each time you play.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

4. Information collected by third parties

The game includes services from Google that collect information directly from your device under Google's own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to revive once per run, to double shards at the end of a run, or to reroll a perk) and full-screen interstitial ads between runs. According to Google's documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

DataDetails
Device and account identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device
IP addressMay be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device
Product interactionsFor example, app launches, taps, and ad and video views
Diagnostic informationApp and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy use

Ad requests also include basic technical information about your device and the app, such as device type, operating system, language, and app version, so that a compatible ad can be delivered.

Google uses this data for advertising (including personalized ads where you have allowed it), ad measurement, analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. For this data Google acts as an independent controller (see the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms). Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK: Google Play data disclosure

If you buy "Remove Ads", interstitial ads stop and rewarded bonuses are granted without watching an ad.

Google Play (downloads and in-app purchases)

The game is distributed through Google Play, and all in-app purchases, such as "Remove Ads" and any items added later, are processed by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information, order details (for example, order ID, product, price, date) and purchase tokens under its own terms. I may see order records in the Google Play Console, such as the order ID, product, price, date, and the country or region of the purchase. I use them only to provide the purchase, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations. I do not receive your full card number.

  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

Google Play and Android may also give developers aggregated, anonymized statistics and crash/performance reports (for example, through Android vitals) from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google. The game does not add any separate crash-reporting or analytics SDK.

5. How information is used

  • To run the game: saving your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show ads: personalized ads where you have consented or not opted out, and non-personalized or limited ads otherwise.
  • To deliver purchases: unlocking and restoring items you buy.
  • To prevent fraud and keep things working: for example, invalid-traffic detection by Google, and purchase verification.
  • To respond to you: if you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example, tax records for sales, or responding to valid legal requests.

6. Your ad privacy choices

  • In-game privacy choices: where required, the game shows a consent or privacy message from Google's User Messaging Platform when you start it. You can review or change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • Advertising ID: on most Android devices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device). There you can reset or delete your advertising ID. If it is deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID.
  • Google ad settings: you can control ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.
  • Buy "Remove Ads": this stops interstitial ads and lets you receive rewarded bonuses without watching ads.

7. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. It asks for your consent before Google stores or accesses information on your device and uses personal data for ads, as required by Google's EU user consent policy and applicable law.

  • If you consent, Google may show personalized ads.
  • If you don't, Google may show non-personalized ads (based on context such as the app and general location) or "limited ads", which do not use personal data for ad personalization. You can still play the full game.
  • Withdraw or change consent at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

Legal bases. Ad-related processing that uses device storage or personal data relies on your consent. Some processing (for example, fraud prevention and basic ad measurement) may rely on legitimate interests as described in Google's consent message. Delivering purchases relies on performance of a contract. Keeping sales and tax records relies on legal obligation.

Your rights. You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. I don't hold personal data about players beyond any emails you send me. For data Google collects through ads or Google Play, you can exercise your rights directly with Google (see the Google Privacy Policy) or contact me and I will help where I can.

8. US state privacy rights (California, Indiana and other states)

Several US states, including California (CCPA as amended by the CPRA), Indiana, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and others, give residents privacy rights. These may include the right to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or its use for targeted advertising.

  • What is collected: through Google AdMob, the categories are identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (interactions with ads and the app), and approximate geolocation inferred from IP address. These are disclosed to Google for the advertising purposes described above. I do not collect sensitive personal information.
  • Opt out of "sale"/"sharing"/targeted ads: if you are in a supported US state, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game to see Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" message and opt out. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (see section 6).
  • Other requests: email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com. Because I don't have accounts or hold player data, I may not be able to link a request to you. For data held by Google, you may also contact Google directly.
  • No discrimination: you will not be treated differently for exercising your privacy rights.
  • You may use an authorized agent where state law allows. I may need to verify the request.

9. Children

Last Bastion is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In some regions the age of digital consent is higher, up to 16. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to me (for example, by email), contact ranceslabs.support@gmail.com and I will delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device stays until you delete it. Uninstall the game or clear its app data (Android Settings → Apps → Last Bastion → Storage → Clear data). This also removes your local privacy choices, and you'll be asked again where required.
  • Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted. You can ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase records in Google Play Console are kept by Google. I may retain sales records as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Data collected by Google (AdMob and Google Play) is retained under Google's policies. See How Google retains data. You can delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

11. Security

The game does not send personal information to my own servers. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS, according to Google. Purchases are handled by Google Play's secure billing system. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I choose reputable providers and keep the game's SDKs up to date.

12. International transfers

I am based in the United States, and Google processes data in the United States and other countries where it or its service providers operate. When data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, Google relies on legal transfer mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, as described in the Google privacy frameworks page.

13. Sale of data

I do not sell your personal information for money. However, allowing Google to use your advertising ID and related data for personalized advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing", or as "targeted advertising", under some US state laws. That is why the opt-out described in section 8 is offered.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example when new features, platforms such as iOS, analytics or crash reporting, cloud saves, or new in-app purchases are added. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. If changes are significant, I will provide notice in the game or on the Google Play listing where appropriate.

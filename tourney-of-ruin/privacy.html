Privacy Policy: Tourney of Ruin

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version
  • Tourney of Ruin is a single-player tactics game made by an individual developer, Jesse Rances. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no cloud saves and no servers of mine. I don't collect your name, email or other personal information through the game.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. You play against computer opponents, offline.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data (such as your advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with ads) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Purchases (such as the Ad-free Pass) are processed by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • The game is meant for players aged 13 and older.

1. Who I am

Tourney of Ruin ("the game", "the app") is developed and published by Jesse Rances, an individual developer based in Indiana, United States ("I", "me", "my"). For any privacy question or request, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information is accessed, collected, used and shared when you play Tourney of Ruin on Android devices, distributed through Google Play. It also covers the third-party services built into the game. Test builds for other platforms aren't publicly distributed and aren't covered here. I'll update this policy before the game is released on other platforms (such as iOS) or adds new features that handle data (see section 14).

3. Information the game itself handles

I don't directly collect personal information. The game has no user accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. I don't run any servers that receive data from the game. The opponents are computer-controlled and run on your device.

  • Game progress and settings: your current run and battle (including the in-progress battle's moves, so you can resume), Renown, unlocks, cosmetics, the Rally Horn, settings (such as music and sound volume and graphics quality) and ad-pacing counters (for example, how many runs you've completed and when the last full-screen ad was shown, so ads stay within their limits). These are stored in save files on your device only and are not sent to me.
  • Purchase status: when you buy something, the game receives a confirmation and a purchase token from Google Play on your device so it can unlock the item. The game also asks Google Play for your existing purchases, so purchases are restored after a reinstall and refunds are reflected. It keeps a copy of what you own on the device so the game works offline. None of this is sent to me, and I never receive your payment details.
  • Privacy choices: your ad consent or opt-out choices are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform, so they're respected each time you play.

The game doesn't request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It doesn't include any analytics or crash-reporting tool of its own.

4. Information collected by third parties

The game includes services from Google that collect information directly from your device under Google's own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. There are no banner ads. There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch: for example, to retry a lost battle once per run ("Second Wind"), to double the gold from a win, for an extra loot choice, for a free shop reroll, or to try a locked unit for one run. They are always optional.
  • Full-screen interstitial ads that show only between battles (results → camp), with strict limits and none for new players at first.

According to Google's documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

Data Details
Device and account identifiers The Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device
IP address May be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device
Product interactions For example, app launches, taps, and ad and video views
Diagnostic information App and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use, plus ad-delivery diagnostics

Ad requests also include basic technical information about your device and the app (device type, operating system, language and app version), so a compatible ad can be delivered.

Google uses this data for advertising (including personalized ads where you've allowed it), ad measurement, analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. For this data Google acts as an independent controller (see the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms). Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK: Google Play data disclosure

If you buy the Ad-free Pass (or the Supporter Bundle, which includes it), interstitial ads stop, and Second Wind and unit trials are granted without an ad. Other rewarded ads stay available as optional choices.

Google Play (downloads and in-app purchases)

The game is distributed through Google Play. All in-app purchases (the Ad-free Pass, cosmetic packs, the Supporter Bundle and any faction packs added later) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information, order details (for example order ID, product, price and date) and purchase tokens under its own terms. I may see order records in the Google Play Console, such as the order ID, product, price, date and country or region of the purchase. I use them only to provide the purchase, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations. I never receive your full card number.

  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

Google Play and Android may also give developers aggregated statistics and crash or performance reports (for example through Android vitals) from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google. The game doesn't add a separate crash-reporting or analytics tool.

5. How information is used

  • To run the game: saving your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show ads: personalized ads where you've consented or haven't opted out, and non-personalized or limited ads otherwise.
  • To deliver purchases: unlocking, restoring and (after a refund) removing items.
  • To prevent fraud and keep things working: for example, invalid-traffic detection by Google and purchase verification by Google Play.
  • To respond to you: if you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example, tax records for sales, or responding to valid legal requests.

6. Your ad privacy choices

  • In-game privacy choices: where required, the game shows a consent or privacy message from Google's User Messaging Platform when you start it. You can review or change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • Advertising ID: on most Android devices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by device). There you can reset or delete your advertising ID. If it's deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID.
  • Google ad settings: you can control ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.
  • Buy the Ad-free Pass: this stops interstitial ads. Every remaining ad is one you choose to watch.

7. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. It asks for your consent before Google stores or accesses information on your device and uses personal data for ads, as required by Google's EU user consent policy and applicable law.

  • If you consent, Google may show personalized ads.
  • If you don't, Google may show non-personalized ads (based on context such as the app and general location) or "limited ads", which don't use personal data for ad personalization. You can still play the full game.
  • You can withdraw or change your consent at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

Legal bases. Ad-related processing that uses device storage or personal data relies on your consent. Some processing (for example fraud prevention and basic ad measurement) may rely on legitimate interests, as described in Google's consent message. Delivering purchases relies on performance of a contract. Keeping sales and tax records relies on legal obligation.

Your rights. You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). I don't hold personal data about players beyond any emails you send me. For data Google collects through ads or Google Play, you can exercise your rights directly with Google (see the Google Privacy Policy), or contact me and I'll help where I can.

8. US state privacy rights (California, Indiana and other states)

Several US states, including California (the CCPA as amended by the CPRA), Indiana, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and others, give residents privacy rights. These may include the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or its use for targeted advertising.

  • What is collected: through Google AdMob, the categories are identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (interactions with ads and the app) and approximate geolocation inferred from the IP address. These are disclosed to Google for the advertising purposes described above. I don't collect sensitive personal information.
  • Opt out of "sale", "sharing" or targeted ads: if you're in a supported US state, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game to see Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" message and opt out. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (see section 6).
  • Other requests: email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts and I don't hold player data, I may not be able to link a request to you. For data held by Google, you can also contact Google directly.
  • No discrimination: you won't be treated differently for exercising your privacy rights.
  • You may use an authorized agent where state law allows. I may need to verify the request.

9. Children

Tourney of Ruin is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. It isn't part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In some regions the age of digital consent is higher, up to 16. If you believe a child under 13 has given me personal information (for example by email), contact ranceslabs.support@gmail.com and I'll delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device stays until you delete it. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Tourney of Ruin → Storage → Clear data (the wording varies by device). This also removes your locally stored privacy choices, and you'll be asked again where required. Deleting game data can't be undone, because there's no backup. Purchases are kept by Google Play and are restored when you reinstall with the same Google account.
  • Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted. You can ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase records in the Google Play Console are kept by Google. I may keep sales records as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Data collected by Google (AdMob and Google Play) is kept under Google's policies. See How Google retains data. You can delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

How to delete your data, step by step: see https://ranceslabs.github.io/tourney-of-ruin/delete-data.html.

11. Security

The game doesn't send personal information to any server of mine. According to Google, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Purchases are handled by Google Play's secure billing system. Save files stay on your device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I choose reputable providers and keep the game's SDKs up to date.

12. International transfers

I'm based in the United States. Google processes data in the United States and in other countries where it or its service providers operate. When data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, Google relies on legal transfer mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, as described on Google's privacy frameworks page.

13. Sale of data

I don't sell your personal information for money. However, letting Google use your advertising ID and related data for personalized advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing", or as "targeted advertising", under some US state laws. That's why the opt-out in section 8 is offered.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy. Online player-versus-player (PvP) modes may be added in a future update. They would likely involve accounts or sign-in, online data and servers. Other changes could include new platforms (such as iOS), analytics or crash reporting, cloud saves, leaderboards, new ad partners, or releases on other stores. I'll update this policy and give notice in the game and on the store listing before any such change takes effect, and I'll ask for your consent where the law requires it. The "Last updated" date at the top will change with every update.

Delete your data: Tourney of Ruin

App: Tourney of Ruin (Google Play)
Developer: Jesse Rances (individual developer)
Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

  • Tourney of Ruin has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud saves. There's no account to delete.
  • I don't hold any player data. Your progress is saved only on your device and is never sent to me.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress permanently. Steps are below.
  • Ad data is collected and held by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and manage your Google ad settings (section 2).
  • For questions or any other request, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com (section 4).

1. Delete your game data

Your progress, settings, privacy choices and purchase status are stored on your device only. To delete them, do either of the following:

  • Uninstall Tourney of Ruin, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Tourney of Ruin → Storage → Clear data (the wording varies by device).

This permanently erases your local progress. It can't be undone, because there's no backup. It also removes your locally stored ad privacy choices, so you'll be asked again where required. Purchases are kept by Google Play and are restored when you reinstall the game with the same Google account.

2. Reset or delete your advertising ID

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data, such as your advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), app and ad interactions, and diagnostics, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google controls that data, so I can't delete it for you, but you can:

  • Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads; the wording varies by device) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. If it's deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID.
  • Control ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.
  • See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, How Google retains data and the Google Privacy Policy to exercise your rights directly with Google.

3. Change your ad consent

Open the game and go to Settings → Privacy choices. This reopens Google's privacy message, where you can:

  • give or withdraw consent for personalized ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland), and
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information or of targeted advertising (supported US states, through Google's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" message).

You can still play the full game, whatever you choose.

4. Email a question or request

I don't hold any player data, so there's nothing on my side to delete beyond any emails you've sent me. For data held by Google, use the controls in sections 2 and 3, or contact Google directly. For questions or any other request, including deleting emails you've sent me, email ranceslabs.support@gmail.com with "Tourney of Ruin: privacy" in the subject. I'll reply within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts, I may not be able to link a request to you.

What is kept

  • Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted. You can ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase records in the Google Play Console are kept by Google. I may keep sales records as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Data collected by Google (AdMob and Google Play) is kept under Google's policies.

If online features with accounts (such as PvP) are added later, this page will be updated before they launch with a way to delete your account and its data.

See the full privacy policy for details: https://ranceslabs.github.io/tourney-of-ruin/privacy.html
